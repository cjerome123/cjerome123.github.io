    Try it: how a unit price is built

    Inside a DPA

    This follows the template I use. Materials are priced per unit. Equipment and labour are priced as a crew for a day, then divided by the crew's daily output. Indirect costs and taxes go on top. Pick an item and change any number.

    ResourceQtyUnitRate, PHPAmount
    Unit cost of item

    0

    Material quantities follow common Philippine estimating tables (for example, 9 bags of cement per cubic metre of Class A concrete). Rates, crews and outputs are sample figures for illustration, not a quote and not any company's price list. Indirect costs use the margin method: each is D ÷ (1 − rate) − D.
